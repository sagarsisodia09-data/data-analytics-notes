Responsibilities of a Data Analyst
	• Acquiring data from various sources and creating queries to extract needed data.
	• Cleaning, filtering, and organizing data for analysis.
	• Using statistical tools to interpret data and identify patterns.
	• Preparing reports and visualizations to communicate findings.
	• Documenting the data analysis process clearly.
Technical Skills for Data Analysts
	• Proficiency with spreadsheets like Excel or Google Sheets.
	• Experience with statistical and visualization software such as Power BI, Tableau, and IBM SPSS.
	• Knowledge of programming languages like Python, R, and SQL.
	• Ability to work with relational and NoSQL databases and data repositories.
	• Familiarity with Big Data tools like Hadoop, Hive, and Spark.
Functional and Soft Skills
	• Strong statistical knowledge and analytical thinking.
	• Problem-solving and probing skills to understand problems deeply.
	• Data visualization and project management capabilities.
	• Effective communication and collaboration with teams.
Curiosity and intuition to explore data insights and challenge assumptions.
